Labeling guidelines

Label the sentiment the wording expresses on its surface, as a plain reading — ignore whether the headline is sarcastic or real news. Negative: the words describe something bad, or use negative or hostile language. Positive: the words describe something good, or use praising or happy language. Neutral: factual or no clear sentiment. If you hesitate between neutral and a sentiment, choose the sentiment only if the evaluative wording is clear; otherwise neutral. Mark Unsure (U) when you genuinely cannot decide.
