## (b) Novelty verdict

- Controlled GAN vs MTL ablation for sarcasm: none found after about 12 phrasings in Consensus and the web. No negative-transfer study of sentiment on sarcasm found either (INFERRED; absence of evidence). Confidence about 60% that no direct prior exists. Beware: GAN-BERT's own curves show gains vanishing at moderate label counts (VERIFIED: SST-5 about 1,700 labels, MNLI about 2,000, QC-coarse about 1,000; still ahead until 40-50% on 20N and QC-fine). Headlines at 16k labels is far past that, so a null GAN effect is the likely result (INFERRED). A well-run null is publishable but modest.
- Label-quality study of machine aux labels for sarcasm: partially pre-empted by Dai 2021 and probably Chen 2022. Open niche: text-only, BERT, per-sample, controlled noise dial, SSL setting.
- Method (sample-level weighting): low. A skeptical reviewer will see SLGrad/Hwang/AMAL applied to one dataset. Confidence about 20% that it passes as a method contribution (INFERRED).
- Single strongest threat: Chen et al. 2022, because it is unreadable to me and may already do soft machine labels plus an ablation. Verified fallback threat: Dai 2021. First action is to get the Chen PDF (IEEE Access is open access; the student's university library may fetch it).
- Whether the instructor's paper helps or hurts: a GAN-null finding (Zihan-style) reads as criticism of her model. Frame it as "when does each component help".

## (f) Recommended version

Reframe from "method" to a controlled study: "Where does semi-supervised gain in sarcasm detection come from?"
- Key observation (INFERRED from the seed paper): aux labels exist for every headline, so at low budgets the helper heads can train on the whole unlabeled pool while the sarcasm head sees 1%. That makes MTL a semi-supervised mechanism competing with the GAN, which she did not separate.
- RQ1: At 1/5/10/30/100% labels, how much of the gain over BERT comes from (i) GAN, (ii) helper heads trained on the unlabeled pool, (iii) both? Include self-training as a cheap SSL control.
- RQ2: How does helper-label quality matter? Gold-subset audit (about 200 headlines), teacher swap (cardiffnlp vs another sentiment model, GoEmotions vs pysentimiento), and a synthetic corruption dial; compare hard, soft, confidence-weighted, and one cheap per-sample rule (head-gradient SLGrad-lite or teacher-agreement weights).
- RQ3 (IDEA): are aux labels themselves outlet proxies? Mutual information between teacher labels and the sarcasm label (CPU, one afternoon); evaluate on iSarcasmEval and a source-masked headline test.
- Minimum set: BERT; +MTL hard; +MTL soft; +MTL weighted; GAN only; GAN+MTL; self-training; TF-IDF+LR; 5 budgets x 5 seeds on headlines; zero-shot transfer to iSarcasmEval; one RoBERTa replication of the main finding; MI check; gold audit.
- Cite her paper as the starting point and replicate her configuration as the reference arm (satisfies the citation goal).
