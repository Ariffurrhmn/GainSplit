# Chen 2022

Hub: [[00 - Project Context]] · Index: [[Literature Index]] · Source table: [[Deep Dive - Paper A]]

Chen, Lin, Zhang, Li, Liu. Jointly learning sentimental clues and context incongruity for sarcasm detection. IEEE Access 10, 2022, 48292-48300. DOI 10.1109/ACCESS.2022.3169864. CC-BY. Full PDF read 2026-11-08.

## What it does (VERIFIED, read in full)
- Two modules fused by concatenation: a **sentiment concern module** (LSTM with self-attention, vector z_s) and an **incongruity search module** (noun-phrase-centred context chunks, window k=5, BiLSTM, vector z_c). Loss: J + lambda*J_s, lambda=0.5.
- The "soft sentiment label" is a **bag-of-words distribution over an opinion lexicon** (Hu and Liu), computed from the sentence itself. It is self-supervised: no pretrained sentiment model and no annotation. Aux loss is cross-entropy against that distribution (Eq. 3-5).
- Models: GloVe-100 + LSTM, **no transformers by design** ("we do not consider Transformer-based models"). Datasets: Reddit /r/movies and /r/technology, IAC-V1/V2. All fully supervised, small (1.5k to 11k train per class). No semi-supervised setting, no GAN, no label-budget sweep.
- Ablation (Table 3, IAC-V2 and Reddit movies): remove sentiment module, replace BoW by 5-level sentiment annotation, remove incongruity module, remove semantics. Differences vs full model roughly 0.4 to 3 pt.

## Overlap with GainSplit
- **Overlaps:** helper-label construction for sarcasm MTL, and one ablation comparing a BoW "soft" target against annotated sentiment scores (their "BoW+Sentiment Annotation" row: 76.07 F1 vs 77.19 on IAC-V2; 72.05 vs 73.42 on movies, i.e. BoW target beat annotation).
- **Does not overlap:** BERT/GAN-BERT backbone, semi-supervised label budgets, a graded label-quality dial (hard/soft/weighted/teacher swap/synthetic noise), gold audit, transfer to iSarcasmEval, outlet-proxy check, helper heads trained on an unlabeled pool.
- Verdict (INFERRED from the read): **partial pre-emption of RQ2 only, in a different regime** (LSTM, supervised, lexicon target). Not a blocker; must be cited and contrasted. Their finding that a lexicon BoW target beats annotated labels is a useful hypothesis for our "lexicon" arm.

## Use in the paper
- Cite as prior work on self-supervised sentiment auxiliary targets for sarcasm.
- Their baselines BiLSTM-MTL (Savini and Caragea) and SAWS are on other datasets; not comparable to Headlines numbers.
